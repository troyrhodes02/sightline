Design handoff · v1.0

An instrument that timestamps its own readings — and says when the needle is old.

This pitch adds projection age and two distinct staleness states to every slate row and contract detail, and replaces the Health placeholder with the admin's three pipeline signals. It deliberately ships no new routes, no mutations, and no retry controls. The commitment to check every frame against: stale is amber caution, predates inactives is neutral disclosure — and the two clocks never merge. Use the Light / Dark switch to flip every product surface.

Note This preview is a rendering of the design, not product code. The application is built in Material UI against the theme; nothing here is copied into it.

What these screens are built to respect

The design commitments a reviewer should check the frames above against.

Currency & staleness

  • Two states, two treatments. Stale is amber caution and clearable by the pipeline; predates inactives is neutral disclosure and must never read as failure. They can co-occur and are never collapsed.
  • The two clocks never merge. Projection age and price age each render beside their own value, at every width, in every state including Kalshi degraded.
  • Staleness lives on rows and is server-computed. Chips are per-game by construction, never dropped at any width, and the client does no clock math.
  • Clearing is silent and honest. A recompute landing clears chips in place via the existing poll; no toast — the timestamps advancing are the feedback.
  • Missing is not stale. No-projection rows carry no age and no staleness chip; unresolved contracts keep their own treatment.

Health discipline

  • Admin-only, structurally. Viewers see no Health nav item, no route, no payload — absence, never a disabled placeholder.
  • Signals move on completed successful runs only. A started-and-died run moves nothing; an empty successful run is success.
  • Partial success is partial. Per-source ingest detail and per-game completeness render whenever they carry information — never one green aggregate over a red detail.
  • The surface reports; it never operates. No retry, no run-now, no logs, no credentials, no links to CI — recovery is command-line only.
  • Quiet in June. Dormant jobs are neutral `not expected`; the only offseason amber is a keepalive that missed its deadline.

Design handoff · v1.0

The instrument that grades itself in public and its operator in private.

The shared accuracy surface answers whether stated probabilities matched observed outcomes — every figure carrying its record, population, and both denominators. The admin-only overrides surface reports took, faded, and skipped descriptively, with timing cost signed so positive means acting early cost value. Nothing here fits a correction, computes a stake, or triggers a run. Use the Light / Dark switch to flip every product surface.

Note This preview is a rendering of the design, not product code. The application is built in Material UI against the theme; nothing here is copied into it.

What these screens are built to respect

The design commitments a reviewer should check the frames above against.

Measurement honesty

  • Two denominators, always. Every calibration figure carries threshold observations and distinct projections — "1,847 obs · 412 projections" is the atom of the surface.
  • Populations are named. Contract-like, all projections, and market-linked are selectable; the market panel pins to market-linked and says so.
  • Unlike metrics never share a frame. Baselines live in the error panel (MAE/RMSE); Brier and reliability live in calibration; backtest and live are separate labelled records, era split disclosed.
  • Small samples look small. Buckets below the 1,000-observation floor render hollow and dashed; the market edge never renders without its interval; exclusions are counted on screen.
  • Two truths, preserved. Official result and Kalshi settlement are stored and shown separately; a disagreement is a displayed fact with both values retained.

Privacy and restraint

  • The private layer is absent, not hidden. A viewer's accuracy page has no Overrides entry, no disabled tabs, no blurred panels; the route rejects server-side before any shell renders.
  • Descriptive, never causal. Overrides states the selection bias plainly; a skip is no action; won/lost carry glyphs and words, never green and red fills.
  • Nothing here operates. No control triggers a backtest, regrade, or refresh; health reports three grading signals and offers no retry button.
  • Colour carries source. Indigo for the model's curve and figures, mint for the market's, neutral for baselines, amber for caution only — never for a bad score.
  • Numerics are mono and tabular, never bold. Timing cost carries an explicit sign and its convention sentence wherever it renders.

Design handoff · v1.0

The strongest opportunities in one glance, one player at a time — honest about what it is worth and what it is not.

The Slate is re-shaped around games and players instead of a repeated-contract list; a player appears once per game with thresholds and stat types inside. Prop Research answers any stored line as a probability, never an edge it cannot compute. Accuracy and the operational tools move behind the admin boundary. Use the Light / Dark switch to flip every product surface.

Note This preview is a rendering of the design, not product code. The application is built in Material UI against the theme; nothing here is copied into it.

What these screens are built to respect

The design commitments a reviewer should check the frames above against.

Hierarchy & honesty

  • One player, one place. A player is a single card per game; thresholds and stat types live inside it, never as sibling rows.
  • Progressive disclosure. Distribution charts render only in detail; collapsed cards carry the summary, never the wall of numbers.
  • Best ≠ likeliest. Ranking is confidence-adjusted edge; a strong "below" ranks with a strong "above."
  • Probability, not worth. Prop Research shows above/below and never an edge number without a real market price.

Provenance, freshness & access

  • Two sources, visibly distinct. Indigo is Sightline's model, mint is Kalshi's market; nothing model-derived wears mint.
  • Price ≠ projection freshness. Five plain-language states; a fresh price never clears a stale projection.
  • Absence, not a locked door. Relocated admin surfaces are absent from viewer markup and rejected server-side.
  • Understandable before technical. Accuracy answers plain questions with denominators first; Brier and the reliability curve remain underneath.

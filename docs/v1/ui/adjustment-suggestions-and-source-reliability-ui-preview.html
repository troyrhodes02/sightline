What these screens are built to respect

The design commitments a reviewer should check the frames above against.

Trust stays manual; both sides stay graded

  • Nothing auto-accepts. Pending reads as unresolved information; no countdown, no auto-apply, no "trusted source" toggle anywhere.
  • Base and shadow both graded. Declining still grades the shadow; accepting never destroys the base. History shows both.
  • The pre-game record freezes at kickoff. A late report is retained and flagged, never edits the frozen prediction.

Two numbers, honest samples, private analytics

  • Source ≠ Adjustment accuracy. Two tiles, two denominators, never merged; an explainer states they measure different things.
  • Small samples look small. Below 15 observations the percentage is withheld and the count shown — "3 of 3", never "100.0%".
  • Admin-only, absent for viewers. The whole Suggestions section is server-rejected for viewers; the only viewer artifact is the accepted-adjustment reason line.
